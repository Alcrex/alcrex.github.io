I’ve made a lot of wrong calculations about my life—like math problems, like misjudging the distance I’ve traveled across a city, and like wondering whether the depth of what I’ve given to certain people was ever worth it.

Just like two years ago, when I broke up with Cheung, I felt like I simply couldn’t take it anymore. But this time is different. Back then, I met Yan, who flew to see me after we had known each other for only half a month. Somehow, that crashed through the pain Cheung had left behind. Although, in the end, life is just a fucking movie.

I just want to do the things I want to do, and do them well.

“Yeah, let’s do it again—but this time, uh, let’s go full psycho.”

I’ve accepted that there are flaws in the way I am—that some parts of my personality are simply not perfect, and that they may never completely disappear.
